# PASSAGE

**Partnership for Atlantic Slavery Scholarship, Archiving and Global Exchange**

PASSAGE is a collaborative research programme led by **The National Archives (UK)** and funded by **Lloyd’s Register Foundation**.

The programme explores histories of enslavement and the transatlantic slave trade through archival collections, with a particular focus on British slave ships between 1760 and 1807.

PASSAGE brings together collections-based research with an international research mobility programme that centres and financially supports West African and Caribbean archivists and researchers working on the history of transatlantic slavery.

Learn more about the [PASSAGE project at The National Archives](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/professional-guidance-and-services/our-research-and-academic-collaboration/our-research-projects/collections-and-archives-research/passage/).

## Digital collections

### Bedford — Voyages, 1804–1807

The **Bedford** digital collection provides an interactive way to explore archival material relating to the voyages of the British slave ship *Bedford* between 1804 and 1807.

Using the [International Image Interoperability Framework (IIIF)](https://iiif.io/), the collection brings together digitised archival records, searchable textual content and interactive viewing tools.

**[Explore the Bedford digital collection](https://passage-tna.github.io/bedford/)**

[View the Bedford GitHub repository](https://github.com/PASSAGE-TNA/bedford)

## Digital research and open technologies

This GitHub organisation hosts digital resources and experimental tools developed to support the exploration, interpretation and presentation of archival material associated with PASSAGE.

The Bedford collection demonstrates the use of open web standards and technologies, including:

- **IIIF** for interoperable access to digital collections.
- **Tamerlane** for viewing and navigating digitised archival material.
- **AnnoSearch** for full-text searching across IIIF resources.
- **Web Annotations** for connecting transcriptions and other information to archival images.

## Content notice

These collections contain historical records relating to enslavement, violence and exploitation. Some documents use offensive or discriminatory language reflecting the historical contexts in which they were created.

The material is presented to support historical research and critical engagement with the archival record.
